Notice of Purpose

Issued under Section 5 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Last updated: 26 April 2026.

What this notice tells you

This notice describes the personal data the PMS Portal collects, the purposes for which it is processed, the legal basis for processing, and the rights you have over that data.

3. Purposes

  1. Performance management of officers.
  2. Project assignment, milestone tracking, and revenue recognition.
  3. Financial reconciliation with NPC's accounting system (Tally).
  4. Reporting to senior management and statutory auditors.
  5. Security incident investigation.

4. Legal basis

Processing is permitted under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023:

  • Section 7(b) — for the performance of any function under any law in force, or for the provision of any service or benefit by the State.
  • Section 7(c) — for the discharge of any obligation under law to disclose information.
  • Section 7(g) — for the purpose of employment.

Because processing is based on these legal grounds and not on consent, you may not "withdraw consent" to stop processing. You retain the access, correction, and grievance rights described below.

5. Retention

  • Audit logs: 180 days (CERT-In requirement).
  • Officer records: duration of employment plus statutory retention (typically 8 years).
  • Backups: 30 days rolling; longer for statutory archives.

6. Recipients

The data is accessed only by authorised NPC officers per their assigned role (officer / Team Leader / Group Head / DDG / DG / system administrator). It is not shared with any third party except where required by law or by the discharge of NPC's mandate.

7. Your rights

  • To access the personal data NPC holds about you.
  • To request correction of inaccurate data.
  • To request erasure of data, subject to retention obligations.
  • To raise a grievance with NPC's Grievance Officer (see Privacy Policy).
  • To approach the Data Protection Board of India if your grievance is not resolved.

8. Security

NPC implements organisational and technical safeguards including encrypted communications, authenticated access, audit logging, daily backups, and restricted administrative access. In the event of a personal data breach, NPC will notify the Data Protection Board and affected individuals in accordance with the Act.
