Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 April 2026

1. Who we are

This portal is operated by the National Productivity Council (NPC), an autonomous organisation under the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade, Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India.

2. What data we collect

This is an internal portal used by NPC officers to record and report their professional work. We collect:

  • Identity: officer ID, full name, email address, phone number, designation, office posting.
  • Activity: assignments you are part of, work-orders, milestones, invoices, payments, expenditure.
  • Audit: timestamps, IP address, and changes you make in the portal (for accountability and CERT-In compliance).

3. Why we collect it

We process this data for the performance of NPC's functions related to officer engagement, performance management, project assignment tracking, and financial reporting. This processing is permitted under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP) Section 7(b) and Section 7(c) (employment-related processing and discharge of legal obligations).

4. How long we keep it

  • Audit logs and login records: 180 days (per CERT-In Direction No. 20(3)/2022-CERT-In, 28 April 2022).
  • Officer profile data: retained for the duration of employment and as required by HR and audit policies thereafter.
  • Backup files: 30 days by default, longer if statutory retention applies.

5. Who can see your data

  • You can see your own profile, your assignments, and your activity history.
  • Your Group Head / Regional Director can see assignments under their office.
  • Senior management (DDG, DG) can see organisation-wide data through dashboards.
  • System administrators have access for technical maintenance only — usage is audit-logged.

6. Where the data lives

The portal and all backups are hosted on infrastructure located within India, in compliance with DPDP Section 16 (data localisation).

7. Your rights under DPDP

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data (write to the contact below).
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request erasure, subject to overriding retention obligations (HR, audit, statutory).
  • Lodge a grievance with the Data Protection Board if your rights are infringed.

8. Security

The portal uses HTTPS with valid TLS, login lockout against brute-force attempts, audit logging of state-changing actions, and daily encrypted backups. The system administrator follows the escalation matrix for any suspected incident, with 6-hour CERT-In notification where applicable.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change. Material changes will be notified inside the portal.
