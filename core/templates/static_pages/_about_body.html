About the PMS Portal

Purpose

The Performance Management System (PMS) Portal is a digital platform for tracking the work and revenue of officers of the National Productivity Council. It captures assignments, training programmes, and non-revenue activities; tracks physical and financial progress against milestones; and produces the management information for senior leadership.

Operated by

National Productivity Council (NPC), an autonomous organisation under the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade, Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India.

Scope

  • Internal-only portal for NPC officers and management.
  • Single source of truth for officer performance data.
  • Integrates with Tally for financial reconciliation.

Built with

  • Django (web framework, Python)
  • MariaDB (database)
  • Self-hosted assets (no external CDNs) for GIGW 3.0 compliance
  • Hosted on infrastructure located within India for DPDP compliance

Compliance

The portal aims to comply with:

  • Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW) 3.0
  • Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA
  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023
  • CERT-In Direction No. 20(3)/2022-CERT-In on cybersecurity practices
